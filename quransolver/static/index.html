QuranSolver

Reflect on what you are going through in the light of the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah.

This app is not a mufti. It shares texts for reflection and does not issue religious rulings. For fiqh questions, consult a qualified scholar. For health, safety or mental-health concerns, please seek professional help as well.

Only verses of the Qur'an and hadith graded authentic (Sahih/Hasan) are shown, and the text is always taken from the database, never generated.
